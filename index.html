About Me

The teacher behind the desk

A little bit about my journey as an elementary and ECD teacher, my classroom, and what I care about most: helping every child thrive.

My Teaching Philosophy

I believe learning sticks when it's hands-on, a little messy, and connected to real life. As an elementary teacher with a background in early childhood development, my classroom runs on routines, read-alouds, and a lot of encouragement — every child leaves my room believing they're capable of hard things.

Outside of teaching, you'll find me hiking with my dog, trying new recipes, or reorganizing my classroom library for the hundredth time.

My Future Plan

A Daycare for Young Mothers in Kibera

My future plan is to open a safe, affordable, and caring daycare in Kibera to support young mothers and their children. The daycare will provide a trusted place for children to learn, play, eat, and grow while their mothers work, study, run a business, or look for opportunities to improve their lives.

Why I Want to Start This Daycare

  • 01 Provide affordable and reliable childcare for young mothers.
  • 02 Create a safe, clean, loving, and child-friendly environment.
  • 03 Help young mothers continue with education, work, or business.

I also want to support families in the community and help reduce the stress that parents experience when they do not have safe and reliable childcare.

What the Daycare Will Offer

  • Safe supervision Children
  • Early learning & play Learning
  • Healthy meals & snacks Nutrition
  • Warm, caring environment Care
  • Support & guidance Mothers

My Vision

I hope to build a daycare that gives children a strong start in life while empowering young mothers to pursue their dreams with confidence and peace of mind.

My Mission

To serve the Kibera community by offering affordable childcare, promoting early childhood development, and supporting young mothers in building a better future for themselves and their children.
