Field trip permission slips are out

Classroom June 20, 2026 · Ms. Mercy

We're heading to the science museum next month! Permission slips went home in Friday folders this week, and we'd love to have every student join us.

The trip is scheduled for a full school day, and we'll be visiting the planetarium, the hands-on physics hall, and the live animal exhibit. Please return signed slips by the date noted at the top of the form.

What to bring

  • A packed lunch and water bottle (no glass containers)
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • A light jacket — the planetarium runs cool

Chaperones needed

We're looking for three to four parent chaperones for the day. If you're interested, please note it on the bottom of the permission slip or send me a quick email.

As always, reach out if the form didn't make it home or if you have any questions before the trip.
