The Little Sprouts Nursery Project

A proposal to build a warm, hands-on nursery learning space for children ages 3–5 — and how you can help make it real.

Proposal Est. launch: 2026

The idea

The Little Sprouts Nursery Project is a plan to turn an underused room at Maple Street Elementary into a dedicated early-years learning space for children ages 3–5, ahead of them joining primary school. The goal is a room built entirely around how young children actually learn: through play, movement, story, and touch.

Why it matters

Many of the children arriving in kindergarten in our community haven't had access to structured early learning. A dedicated nursery space — with the right materials, a trained routine, and a safe environment — helps close that gap before it widens, and gives families in our area an affordable, nearby option.

What we're building

  • A reading corner with a rug, low shelving, and leveled picture books
  • A sensory and fine-motor station with washable play materials
  • An art and messy-play area with child-height tables
  • Basic safety upgrades: soft flooring, corner guards, and shelving anchors

Budget snapshot

We're aiming to raise KES 250,000 to cover furniture, learning materials, and safety upgrades for the first cohort of 40 children. Every contribution — large or small — goes directly toward the room.

Volunteer with us

We welcome help with room setup and painting, sewing or donating soft furnishings, reading to children once the space opens, and general classroom support during the school day. No experience with young children is required — just patience and a willingness to get a little messy.

To volunteer, reach out through the contact form on the homepage or email mercy.classroom@example.com with "Sprouts Volunteer" in the subject line, and let us know what kind of time or skills you can offer.
