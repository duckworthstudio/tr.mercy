New read-aloud: Charlotte's Web

Reading July 2, 2026 · Ms. Mercy

We just turned the first page of our newest chapter book: E.B. White's Charlotte's Web. Room 12 read the opening chapter together on the carpet, and by the second page half the class already had opinions about Wilbur.

We'll read a chapter or two most afternoons after lunch, and I'll be sending home a short set of discussion questions each week so families can keep the story going at the dinner table if they'd like to.

Why this book

Charlotte's Web is a gentle way to talk about friendship, change, and looking out for one another — themes that come up naturally in a third-grade classroom. It also gives us plenty to write about during our journaling block.

"Wait, can spiders even write words?" — the question that kicked off our best classroom discussion this month.

What's next

We'll finish the book over the next several weeks, and each student will put together a small project about their favorite character once we reach the final chapter. More details on that soon.

If your family already has a copy at home, your child is welcome to follow along — just let me know so I can keep them in sync with our classroom pace.
